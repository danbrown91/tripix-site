Tripix Support

Troubleshooting Tips

If something doesn’t look quite right, here are a few things you can check.

Trip Detection

Trips Aren’t Being Detected

If your trips aren’t being detected, it’s usually because there isn’t enough information in your photos.

Tripix relies on location data stored in your photos. If location services were turned off when the photos were taken, they may not include the data needed to identify trips.

You can also try increasing your Sensitivity Level or re-scanning your photo library.

Location Data

Photos Have No Location Data

Some photos may not include location information.

This can happen if:

  • Location services were disabled
  • The photo was edited or exported
  • The image was downloaded from another source

Without location data, Tripix cannot determine where the photo was taken.

Trip Accuracy

Trips Look Incorrect

If a trip doesn’t look quite right, it may be due to gaps or inconsistencies in your photo data.

For example, missing timestamps or locations can affect how photos are grouped.

You can always review and adjust your trips before confirming them to ensure everything looks right.

Home Location

Home Location Issues

If Tripix is detecting trips incorrectly, your address history may need updating.

Your home location is used to determine whether a photo was taken “at home” or “away”.

Make sure your address history is accurate and covers the correct time periods.

Sensitivity

Sensitivity Feels Off

If too many trips are being detected, try lowering your Sensitivity Level.

If not enough trips are being detected, try increasing it.

This setting controls how far from home you need to travel before something is considered a trip.

Scanning

Scanning Takes Time

Scanning your photo library can take time, especially if you have a large number of photos.

Processing happens on your device to protect your privacy, which means it may take longer than cloud-based services.

You can leave the app open while scanning completes.

Permissions

Permissions Not Working

If Tripix isn’t working as expected, check your permissions.

Make sure the app has access to:

  • Photos
  • Location (if required)

You can update permissions at any time in your device settings.
