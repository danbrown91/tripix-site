Photo Settings

Your Photos, your control.

Photo Access

Photo Access

Tripix needs access to your photo library to understand where and when your photos were taken.

We only use metadata stored within your photos, such as location and time. Your photos themselves are never uploaded or shared.

If access is not granted, Tripix won’t be able to automatically detect trips for you.

How it works

How Your Photos Are Used

Your photos are analysed on-device to identify patterns in location and time.

This allows Tripix to group photos into trips, calculate distances travelled, and generate insights about your journeys.

Everything happens locally on your device — your data stays with you.

Automatic Trip Detection

Automatic Trip Detection

When enabled, Tripix automatically scans your photos to detect trips.

It looks for clusters of photos taken away from your home location and groups them into potential trips for you to review.

You’ll always be asked to confirm trips before anything is finalised.

Re-scan

Re-Scan Photo Library

If you’ve recently added new photos or updated your settings, you can re-scan your library at any time.

This will reprocess your photos and update any detected trips and insights.

Depending on the size of your library, this may take a little time.

Camera Access

Camera Access

Tripix may request access to your camera to allow you to capture photos directly within the app.

Photos taken using the camera can be included in your trips, just like those from your library.

Privacy

Privacy & Control

Your privacy matters.

Tripix does not upload your photos or personal data to external servers. Everything is processed and stored locally on your device.

You can change photo permissions at any time in your device settings.
