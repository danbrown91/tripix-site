Support

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about Tripix, your photo library, travel detection and subscriptions.

How does Tripix find my trips?

Tripix uses information already stored with your photos, including dates and available location metadata, to identify periods of travel and organise them into trips.

Why does Tripix need access to my photos?

Photo access allows Tripix to analyse the dates and locations associated with your images so it can build your travel history and display the photos connected with each trip.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Tripix is designed to analyse your photo library on your device. Your photos do not need to be uploaded to a Tripix server in order for Tripix to identify your travels.

Why hasn't Tripix detected one of my trips?

Trip detection depends on the date and location information available in your photo library. Trips with very few photos, missing location data, or unusual travel patterns may occasionally need a rescan or adjustment.

Can I rename a trip?

Yes. Trip names can be customised in the app without changing the original photos in your library.

What is included with Tripix Premium?

Tripix Premium unlocks importing and rescanning new photos, future-trip detection and premium sharing features, together with additional premium functionality as Tripix evolves.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through the App Store are managed through your Apple ID subscription settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; access remains available until the end of the current subscription period.

How do I restore my subscription?

Use the restore purchases option within Tripix while signed in to the Apple ID originally used for the purchase.
