Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

Getting Started

What does Tripix do?

Tripix helps you visualise your travel history on a map and explore stats about the journeys you’ve been on.

Why do I need to add my address history?

Your address history helps Tripix understand where you were based at different points in your life, improving trip detection accuracy.

Travel Detection

How does Travel Detection work?

Tripix uses your photos, timestamps, and location clues to help identify trips and place them correctly on your map.

Why is a trip missing?

Trips can be harder to detect when there are gaps in your photo library, missing metadata, or incomplete address history.

Photos & Library

Do all my photos need location data?

No, but having more photos with accurate timestamps and location metadata helps Tripix build a more complete picture of your journeys.

Privacy & Data

Is my data private?

Your travel data and personal information should be handled according to your app’s privacy design and policy. You can tailor this wording to exactly match your actual implementation.

Troubleshooting

Why are my stats not updating?

Try checking that your photo permissions are enabled, your address history is complete, and any trip scanning or refresh process has finished successfully.
