Tripix

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Introduction

Tripix is designed to help you discover, organise and visualise your travel history using information from your photo library.

Privacy is central to how Tripix works. Your photos, photo metadata and personal travel history are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to Tripix servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tripix accesses, how it is used, and when limited information may be processed by third-party services that support the app.

Photo Library and Travel Data

With your permission, Tripix accesses your photo library to identify trips and generate travel information.

Depending on the information available in your photo library, Tripix may use:

  • Photo and video timestamps
  • Location metadata associated with photos and videos
  • Other metadata required to organise your library and identify trips
  • Photos and videos for displaying your travel history within the app

Tripix uses this information to provide features such as:

  • Identifying and organising trips
  • Displaying trips and visited locations
  • Generating travel statistics, insights and achievements
  • Selecting and displaying photos associated with your trips
  • Creating content that you choose to save or share

Your photos, photo metadata and generated travel history are processed locally on your device. Tripix does not upload your photo library or travel history to Tripix servers.

Address Information

Tripix may allow you to provide information about places you have lived. This information is used locally to help distinguish travel from time spent at home and improve the accuracy of your travel history.

Address information you provide is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to Tripix servers.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Tripix offers optional purchases and subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Tripix uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to manage subscription status and determine access to premium features.

When you make or restore a purchase, limited purchase and subscription information is processed by RevenueCat. Tripix uses RevenueCat's anonymous identifiers and does not provide RevenueCat with your name or email address for this purpose.

RevenueCat may also use purchase information to provide subscription management and related analytics functionality in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Payments themselves are processed by Apple. Tripix does not receive or store your payment card or other payment-account details.

Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Tripix may send local notifications relating to features such as previous trips, travel memories and other app features.

You can change notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Data Storage

Tripix does not maintain a centralised database containing your photo library, address history or personal travel history.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device.

Removing Tripix from your device will generally remove locally stored Tripix data, subject to the behaviour of your device, operating system and any device backups.

Third-Party Services

Tripix uses limited third-party services where necessary to provide particular functionality.

These may include services provided by Apple as part of iOS and the App Store, and RevenueCat for managing purchases and subscriptions.

Third-party services may process limited information necessary to provide their functionality and operate according to their own privacy policies.

Tripix does not sell your personal data and does not use your personal travel information or photo library for advertising.

Tracking and Advertising

Tripix does not use your personal information for targeted advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes.

Your Choices

You remain in control of the permissions granted to Tripix.

You can:

  • Manage Tripix's access to your photo library through your device settings
  • Manage notification permissions through your device settings
  • Update or remove address information within Tripix
  • Choose whether to purchase or maintain a Tripix subscription

If you withdraw a permission, functionality that relies on that permission may become unavailable or limited.

Data Retention

Locally generated Tripix data is retained on your device for as long as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

Purchase and subscription information processed by Apple and RevenueCat may be retained according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

Tripix does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Tripix-operated online service.

The core personal information used to generate a user's travel history is processed locally on the user's device rather than collected by Tripix.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Tripix develops or if its data practices change.

When this happens, the date at the top of this policy will be updated. Where appropriate, significant changes may also be communicated within the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Tripix handles information, please contact Tripix support.
