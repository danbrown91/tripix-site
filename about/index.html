About Tripix

Travel, thoughtfully rediscovered.

Tripix quietly keeps track of where life takes you.

Using the moments in your photo library, Tripix uncovers the journeys you’ve already lived — weekends away, long-haul adventures, and everything in between — and brings them together into one beautifully organised timeline.

No planning. No logging. No effort required.
Just your travels, thoughtfully rediscovered.

Unlike traditional travel apps, Tripix focuses on discovery rather than planning — intelligently analysing your photos to uncover trips you may have forgotten, and presenting them in a way that feels effortless and natural.

What’s yours stays yours

Private by design.

With Tripix, your data never leaves your device and is only used to build your personal travel timeline.
